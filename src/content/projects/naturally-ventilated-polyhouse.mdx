Protected cultivation lets farmers extend seasons and protect high-value crops from heavy rain, pests and temperature extremes. A **naturally ventilated polyhouse** keeps costs and energy use low by relying on roof and side vents instead of fans.

## Design considerations

### Orientation and ventilation

Structures are oriented to take advantage of prevailing winds, with roof vents and roll-up side curtains sized to promote stack and wind-driven ventilation.

### Structure

- Galvanised iron (GI) pipe frames sized for local wind and snow loads.
- Foundations and anchoring suited to the site's soil conditions.
- UV-stabilised polyethylene film with insect-proof netting at openings.

### Nursery layout

High-tech nurseries add raised benches, misting and shading, organised for efficient workflow from seeding to hardening.

## Detailed estimation

Every design is accompanied by a **bill of quantities**, rate analysis and cost estimate following government norms, so the drawings can move straight into subsidy and procurement processes.

## Tools

AutoCAD for drawings, SketchUp for 3D visualisation, and spreadsheet-based estimation and costing.

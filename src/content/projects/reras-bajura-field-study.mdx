The **Renewable Energy for Resilient Agriculture System (RERAS)** project looks at how clean energy can strengthen farming in climate-vulnerable, remote districts. I joined the field team in Bajura to collect data and to train farmers.

## Scope of work

- Conducted the **field study** across project sites in Bajura.
- Delivered **training on agricultural machinery** and climate-resilient farming systems.
- Prepared **questionnaires** for focus group discussions, key informant interviews and household surveys.
- Designed the **methodology and data analysis framework**.
- Compiled **technical and scientific reports** from the findings.

## Research design

### Mixed methods

Quantitative household surveys were paired with qualitative focus groups and key-informant interviews, so patterns in the numbers could be explained by farmers' own accounts.

### Gender-responsive lens

A literature review on **climate-smart and gender-responsive technologies** shaped the survey instruments, making sure questions captured who does the work, who makes decisions and who benefits from new technology.

## Analysis

Survey data was cleaned and analysed with statistical and machine-learning techniques in Python to identify patterns and predictors of technology adoption.

## Outcomes

Findings fed into project reports and were later shared at a stakeholder consultation workshop on climate-resilient and inclusive agriculture organised by ICIMOD and UNDP.

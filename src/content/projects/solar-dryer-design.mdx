A large share of fruits and vegetables is lost between harvest and market. **Solar drying** is one of the most affordable ways to turn perishable surplus into shelf-stable products.

## Approach

### Choosing a dryer type

- **Direct (cabinet) dryers**: simplest and cheapest, suitable for products that tolerate sunlight.
- **Indirect dryers**: air is heated in a collector and passed over the produce, preserving colour and nutrients.
- **Mixed-mode dryers**: combine both for faster drying.

### Sizing

Dryer capacity is matched to the expected batch size, the moisture to be removed and local solar radiation, with airflow designed to carry moisture out efficiently.

## Linking with value addition

Dryers are paired with simple processing (slicing, blanching, packaging) and with **cold storage** where appropriate, so farmers can choose the right preservation route for each crop.

## Status

Design and estimation work is ongoing, together with farmer training on hygienic handling and drying practice.
